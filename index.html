Apps

Six little tools for everyday life. Install them on your phone, or open them here.

Hours & Days Where the week actually goes: work, the bench, the reading, and the plan against it. Expense Tracker What comes in, what goes out, and how much of the reserve is left. Admissions Every PhD application, its documents and letters, deadlines and replies. Italiano A little Italian, every day. Vocabulary, grammar, verbs and sentences that teach you. Reading Room Your Zotero bookshelf, chapter progress and private on-device reminders. GRE Quant Quest Practice GRE Quant with questions, formulas, field notes and memory cards.

On an iPhone, open one in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen. It installs with its own icon and opens without a browser bar. Italiano and Reading Room keep progress on each device; use their Progress tabs to export or restore a backup. The other apps keep separate data until you turn sync on — tap the sync line in the app (bottom of the side rail, or the "Sync" pill on a phone) and paste a GitHub token to link it to your other devices, or use Backup to move a copy over by hand.
