# Fonts

Archivo, JetBrains Mono, Zilla Slab, IBM Plex Mono, Source Sans 3 and Spectral are distributed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org). The files are unmodified WOFF2 copies of the Latin and Latin Extended subsets served by Google Fonts. They are hosted here so that the apps load no resources from outside services.
